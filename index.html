FinAI – Privacy & Terms

FinAI is a private, single-user application operated by its owner for personal use only. It is not offered to the public.

Privacy

FinAI reads only the owner's own accounts (read-only: balances and transactions). It does not initiate payments. Data is stored only on the owner's private system and is not shared with, sold to, or processed by third parties.

Terms

There are no other users. Use is limited to the owner.
